# Legal pages: fill in before going public

Pages: `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `refund.html`, `contact.html` (live at /privacy, /terms, /refund, /contact).
Links appear at the bottom of the left panel and under the payment dialog.

Replace these 5 placeholders in all four files (use your editor's Find & Replace across files):

| Placeholder | Put |
|---|---|
| `{{BUSINESS}}` | Your name or business name |
| `{{SITE}}` | Your site address, e.g. https://yourbrand.in |
| `{{EMAIL}}` | Support email |
| `{{CITY}}` | City (used for jurisdiction and the contact page) |
| `{{HOURS}}` | e.g. Mon-Sat, 10am-7pm IST |

Check the numbers match your real policy: 3 free exports, 3 working days to activate, 7 days refund window, 5-7 days to pay out.
Update "Last updated" if you change anything. These are good-faith templates, not legal advice: have a lawyer or CA review them before you start charging.
