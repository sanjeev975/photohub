The short version

  • Payment not activated within 3 working days: full refund.
  • Charged twice, or the tool did not work for you within 7 days: refund.
  • Refunds go back to your UPI account in 5–7 working days.

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Please read this before paying.

1. Before you pay

Use your free exports to confirm the tool works with your photos and device.

2. When you get a refund

  • You paid, and we could not activate your plan within 3 working days of receiving your UTR.
  • You were charged twice for the same plan.
  • You paid but the Service did not work for you (for example, exports fail) and we could not fix it within a reasonable time. Request this within 7 days of activation.

3. When we do not refund

  • Change of mind after you have exported sheets with your plan.
  • Rejection of your photos by an authority, embassy or portal (check the official rules before applying).
  • Wrong UTR, wrong amount or wrong plan entered by you. Write to us and we will try to correct it, for example by moving the amount to another plan.
  • Accounts suspended for misuse.

4. How to ask

Email {{EMAIL}} with your UTR, the contact you entered and a short description. We reply within 3 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the same UPI account within 5 to 7 working days. Your plan is ended when the refund is made.

6. Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. Your plan simply ends on its expiry date.
