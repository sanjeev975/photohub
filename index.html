Printing Calibration Checklist

⚠️ Common mistake: Most printer dialogs default to "Fit to Printable Area" which shrinks passport photos by 3% to 6%, making them non-compliant.
  1. Scale Setting: Always choose 100% or "Actual Size" in your browser or Adobe Acrobat print dialog.
  2. Paper Matching: Ensure the paper loaded in the tray matches your chosen sheet format (A4 or 4×6 photo paper).
  3. Paper Quality: Use glossy or matte photo paper (recommended 180–240 gsm) rather than standard thin copy paper.
  4. Ruler Verification: After printing, use a physical millimeter ruler to measure your printed photos. A 35×45mm photo must measure exactly 3.5cm wide by 4.5cm high.
