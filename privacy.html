The short version

  • Your photos stay on your device. We never upload or see them.
  • We use one cookie to count free exports and recognise your plan.
  • We never sell your data, and we use no advertising trackers.

{{BUSINESS}} ("we", "us") runs PassportPhoto Hub at {{SITE}}. This page explains what we do and do not collect.

1. Your photos stay on your device

Photos you add are opened, cropped, arranged and exported inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them. When you close the page they are gone from memory.

2. What we do collect

  • A device cookie (pph_did): a random, signed identifier used to count free exports and to recognise your subscription. It is a strictly necessary, first-party cookie, kept for up to 2 years. It is not used for advertising.
  • A photo-set fingerprint: a one-way hash computed in your browser from your photos, used only to tell whether you are exporting the same pictures again. The hash cannot be turned back into a photo.
  • A network fingerprint: a one-way (keyed) hash of your IP address, used to limit abuse of the free trial. We do not store your raw IP address in our database. Our hosting provider may process IP addresses in its normal server logs.
  • Usage counters: number of free exports used, and rate-limit counters.
  • Payment details you give us (only when subscriptions are enabled): the plan chosen, amount, the UPI reference (UTR) you enter, your mobile number or email, and timestamps. We never receive your UPI PIN, bank login or card details.
  • Settings stored in your browser (local storage), such as default sheet settings and panel layout. These stay on your device.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the tool and enforce the free-trial limit.
  • To confirm payments and activate, renew or restore your plan.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to answer support requests.
  • To meet accounting and legal obligations.

4. Third parties

The page loads open-source libraries and fonts from public CDNs (cdnjs, unpkg, jsDelivr, Google Fonts), which can see your IP address and browser details when they serve those files. Our site is hosted on Vercel and our usage and payment records are stored with Upstash (Redis). We do not sell your data and do not use advertising trackers.

5. How long we keep it

Trial counters and rate-limit data expire automatically or are kept only as long as the trial protection needs them. Payment records (UTR, plan, contact, dates) are kept as long as needed for your subscription, support and accounting. You can ask us to delete or correct your data at any time (section 7).

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, cookies are HttpOnly, and access to payment records is limited to the site owner. No online service is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

7. Your rights and contact

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by writing to {{EMAIL}}. We will respond within a reasonable time. For India, this is intended to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Children

Passport photos are often of minors. The tool processes them only on your device. If you are under 18, please use the service with a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
